\title{Constant oracle-risk bounds for normalized spectral likelihood\\
\large End-to-end Bernoulli SBM and degree-corrected SBM results}
\author{Research proof note for the spectral-lrt project}
\date{20 October 2026}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We give finite, implementable classifiers based only on retained normalized
spectral information and prove expected misclassification risk at most a
constant times a precisely specified local oracle risk. For ordinary SBM,
the signed Euclidean spectral reconstruction and finite growing/replacement
likelihood initializer are covered on an explicit nontrivial model class,
with a broader information--degree margin extension. For bounded-degree-parameter
DC-SBM, a degree-metric spectral reconstruction, deterministic spectral
initialization, estimated nuisance parameters and Bernoulli profile likelihood
give the bound for general positive full-rank block profiles when
\(C_0\log n\le np\) and \(np^2\le C_1\). The reference is the full-edge
profile-LRT oracle with true neighbor parameters. Equal block exposures
further imply a bound relative to the oracle supplied with the target's
true degree parameter. A two-point counterexample shows that this stronger
oracle cannot be matched throughout general DC-SBM, even from the full
adjacency matrix. The proof keeps the sharp \(d^{-1/2}\) prefactor,
establishes actual full-graph-to-LOO score control, and pays every failure
event at the oracle scale. The algorithm modifications and model restrictions
are explicit; a bounded risk ratio is not a claim that the ratio converges.
\end{abstract}
