Build from this directory with a standard TeX Live installation:

pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex

The build output main.pdf is distributed as sbm_spectral_likelihood.pdf.
This article has no external TeX dependency on the companion.
The included main.bbl permits a pdfLaTeX-only rebuild when desired.
