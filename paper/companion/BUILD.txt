Build from this directory with TeX Live and BibTeX:

  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
  bibtex main
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex

Copy main.pdf to spectral_likelihood_companion.pdf for distribution.
The first article is referenced by name and a relative PDF hyperlink only.
No external auxiliary file, external-document package, or network access is needed.
The supplied main.bbl also supports a rebuild without BibTeX when citations are unchanged.
