\subsection{Floored-simplex weight updates}
For a fixed floor $f\in[0,1/K)$, the weight M-step solves
\begin{equation}
 \max_{\omega_a\geq f,\,\sum_a\omega_a=1}\sum_aN_a\log\omega_a.
\end{equation}
Its active-set solution starts with every component free. If the free set is $S$, assign mass $1-f(K-|S|)$ proportionally to the $N_a$, $a\in S$, and fix any weight below $f$ at the floor. Repeat on the remaining free set. The KKT conditions give
\begin{equation}
 \omega_a=\max\{f,N_a/\lambda\},\qquad \sum_a\omega_a=1
\end{equation}
for a suitable multiplier $\lambda$. The active-set procedure enforces this equation; it is not a naive clipping followed by renormalization. The tested function \texttt{\_floored\_weights} implements this exact constrained update. Since the E-step and mean M-step are unchanged, the variational proof applies on the floored-simplex parameter domain.

\subsection{An explicit error decomposition}
For fitted components matched to labels $a,b$, put
\begin{equation}
 \widehat w^{ab}_j=\log(\mu_{aj}/\mu_{bj}),\qquad
 \widehat c^{ab}=-\sum_j(\mu_{aj}-\mu_{bj})+\log(\omega_a/\omega_b).
\end{equation}
If $\lambda^{ab}$ is the chosen oracle prior log ratio, the empirical comparison has the decomposition
\begin{align}
 \widehat T_i^{ab}-(L_i^{ab}+\lambda^{ab})
 &=A_i(\Pi-I)w^{ab}
   +(q_i-\widetilde P_i)w^{ab}\nonumber\\
 &\quad+q_i(\widehat w^{ab}-w^{ab})
   +\widehat c^{ab}-c_i^{ab}-\lambda^{ab}.
\label{eq:error_decomposition}
\end{align}
The terms correspond to empirical projection, positivity clipping, estimated contrasts, and intercept calibration. The reconstructed diagonal is included in these terms; the oracle excludes it through $A_{ii}=0$ and the definition of $c_i^{ab}$. For Poisson rather than Bernoulli oracle contrasts, the sparse correction must also be included. Formula~\eqref{eq:error_decomposition} is exact and gives concrete quantities for checking the transfer conditions. The present manuscript does not establish the required simultaneous bounds for the practical EM endpoint.
