# End-to-end likelihood decoder: new proof and explicit algorithm addition

The preceding version proved the empirical spectral fit only after a reliable weak start. That did not establish the primary growing algorithm's full guarantee.

Two new results close concrete algorithmic guarantees:

1. The original global-mean growing algorithm, with its actual all-node candidate scan and finite stopping rule, is proved for K=2. Candidates may be rare extreme rows. A weighted divergence identity forces opposite signs of the two population gain margins, so the first E-step is weakly consistent at arbitrary polynomial confidence.
2. For every fixed K, the complete algorithm retains the growing fit and adds ceil(log(n)) independent likelihood-residual++ fits. Highest working likelihood selection and one final E/M step give an end-to-end leading-exponent theorem.

The general-K addition is part of the stated algorithm. It is not a proof of the unchanged growing branch, and it does not introduce a k-means warm start.

The proof of the complete algorithm has four steps:

- A residual++ run chooses good candidates from all K communities with probability 1-o(1); log(n) independent runs reduce total failure to n^{-omega(1)}.
- The working likelihood equals a common saturation constant minus soft deviance F. One successful initialization gives F=o(n log(n)), which exact EM preserves.
- Highest likelihood selection preserves that bound even if growing wins. Strong convexity and the posterior KL identity then force aligned average responsibility error o(1).
- The mandatory final M-step invokes the already proved actual-spectral EM theorem, giving E Mis <= exp(-(1+o(1)) I_n), and exact recovery when liminf I_n/log(n)>1.

The implementation preserves the original API arm and exposes the complete algorithm as method='global_gain_certified'. Twenty-four fresh simulations verify the selection and final-update logic. They do not replace the archived 240-graph experiment or establish a universal finite-sample accuracy advantage.

The proof uses only retained eigenpairs, no raw adjacency refinement, degree input, true labels, or true P. The rates refer to the leading exponent; Zhou–Li's refined optimal rate is not restated.

## Conditions clarified in the full review of 2026-10-10

The theorem assumes fixed positive symmetric full-rank (n/log n)P and positive limiting proportions. With the implemented logarithm floor 1e-4 log n/n, it also requires min_ab nP_ab/log n > 1e-4. Degrees need not be equal. The graph spectrum is computed before positive profile clipping. The result attains the oracle leading exponent, not a refined multiplicative rate or a new global minimax lower bound. The current manuscript does not include the unproved lower-density extension.
