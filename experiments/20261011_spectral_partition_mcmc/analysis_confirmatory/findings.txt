Confirmatory conditions remain separate from the n=512 main experiment.
Input directories: results_n1024 and results_k6 only.
Six graphs: two each for n=1024 balanced K=2, n=1024 hierarchical K=4,
and n=512 balanced K=6. Two random starts per graph and method.
All conditions achieved oracle Chernoff information I/log(n)=1.35.
Gibbs, ICM and tempering used 2000 sweeps; EM used at most 200 rounds.
No duplicate endpoints and no missing trajectory archives.

Final-state exact recovery:
n=1024 balanced K=2: Gibbs 0/4, Gibbs6 0/4, PT 0/4, EM 0/4,
ICM 0/4, spectral k-means 2/2.
n=1024 hierarchical K=4: Gibbs 2/4, Gibbs6 2/4, PT 2/4, EM 0/4,
ICM 0/4, spectral k-means 2/2.
n=512 balanced K=6: Gibbs 4/4, Gibbs6 4/4, PT 4/4, EM 0/4,
ICM 1/4, spectral k-means 2/2.

Gibbs6 selects its endpoint using the working posterior among six cold
chains, never using truth labels. Best-posterior exact counts happen to
match final-state exact counts in these conditions; they are still saved
and interpreted separately.

The four generated plot panels were visually inspected. Confirmatory
trace plots use graph seed 0 and random start 0 by a metadata-only rule.
They show a long random plateau for balanced K=2 at n=1024, successive
partial recovery plateaus for hierarchical K=4, and fast recovery for K=6.
The endpoint plot combines distinct model columns with distinct sample
sizes: K=2 and K=4 have n=1024; K=6 has n=512.

These are small-sample finite-budget recovery observations. Runs on one
graph are dependent, and successful recovery does not establish mixing.
