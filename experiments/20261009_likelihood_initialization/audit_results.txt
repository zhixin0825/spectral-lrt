Independent audit of frozen SBM likelihood-initialization experiments
===================================================================

Scope and completeness
----------------------
720/720 jobs complete: 6 models x 6 degree levels x 20 paired graph seeds.
n=1800, degrees 4,6,12,24,64,96, final seeds100:120, six restarts per method.
36 complete model/degree conditions. Zero failed records and zero duplicated
method/stage records. Audit uses only final/, never pilot outputs. Algorithms
were not changed or tuned by this audit. Independent reproducible analysis:
audit_final.py; detailed CSVs and metadata: final_audit/.

Same-feature comparisons
------------------------
KMeans/GMM arms use identical feature matrices and the same per-restart center
seeds. Full GMM versus KMeans on standardized spectral coordinates has lower
condition-mean error in 25/36 conditions, a tie in1, and higher error in10.
Full GMM on neighbor+degree features is better in25, tied in3, worse in8.
Poisson-Gaussian (PG) versus neighborhood+degree KMeans is better in27, tied in3,
worse in6. PG uses a different seeding/model specification, so this last
comparison isolates equal available information, not objective alone.

Selected genuine gains against the neighborhood+degree KMeans baseline:
- K3 heterogeneous, d=24: 10.050% -> PG7.519%; paired difference -2.531 percentage
  points, descriptive95%CI[-2.879,-2.182]pp;20/20 paired graph wins.
- K3 heterogeneous, d=12: 19.928% -> PG18.750%; difference -1.178pp,
  CI[-1.736,-0.620]pp;16/20 wins.
- K3 heterogeneous, d=6:43.722% -> PG34.583%; difference -9.139pp,
  CI[-12.022,-6.256]pp;17/20 wins.
- K3 equal-degree, d=24:3.475% -> full GMM2.411% or PG2.425%; each20/20 wins.
- K4 hierarchical, d=96:3.131% -> PG2.197%; difference -0.933pp,
  CI[-1.099,-0.768]pp;20/20 wins.

Counterexamples to uniform improvement:
- K3 equal-degree,d=4: neighborhood+degree KMeans54.356%,PG62.389%.
- K3 disassortative,d=6: same baseline40.478%,PG47.736%.
- K4 hierarchical,d=24: same baseline27.200%,PG28.594%.
- K2 symmetric,d=6: classic-U KMeans15.553%,PG20.669%,full GMM(spec)32.747%.
- K4 equal-degree,d=24: ASE KMeans6.811%,RRE KMeans6.803%,PG12.842%; a large
  gain over the poorer neighborhood+degree KMeans33.725% would hide this.

Geometry control must not be mistaken for a strong standard baseline
-------------------------------------------------------------------
At K2,d=96, the standardized spectral+degree KMeans arm has45.781% mean error,
whereas classic U, ASE, RRE, and full GMM recover all graphs exactly. On seed100,
true communities have mean degrees96.151 and95.789. The bad KMeans partition
instead has means104.000 and88.721. Standardizing the leading-eigenvector
fluctuation and appending standardized degree approximately duplicates noise.
In the illustrative feature model(G,S,G),G standard normal and S independent
equiprobable+/-1, true-community KMeans cost is2, while degree-sign splitting
cost is3-4/pi=1.72676. Thus enormous GMM gains on this representation do not
establish gains over a well-scaled conventional spectral initializer.

Decoder failure is a separate, material result
----------------------------------------------
The common unguarded empirical centroid-inverse decoder is not reliable on
these finite-n experiments. It can destroy an already excellent initialization.
K3 heterogeneous,d=64: PG initial error0.6778%, decoded58.1639%.19/20 graphs
select a negative empirical eigenvalue among top3 by absolute value, although
population C is positive definite. This indicates a weak signal direction has
been displaced by noise. Mean centroid condition number572.5,max3001.1; inferred
counts are negative46.16% of the time. At d=96 all selected eigenvalues are
positive, mean condition1.1606, initial error0.0528%,decoded0.1278%.
Other examples: K4 equal-degree,d=64 PG0.1694% -> decoded45.5694%; K4 hierarchical,
d=96 PG2.1972% -> decoded56.3889%. These are not evidence against an asymptotic
theorem under genuine signal-subspace separation, but they preclude claiming
this frozen decoder empirically attains the oracle LRT here. No guard was tuned
post hoc, and these unfavorable results remain in the report.

Diagnostics
-----------
Across720 selected restarts per method:
- KMeans variants: all meet iteration limit convergence criterion.
- Full GMM(spec):9 nonconverged; full GMM(spec+degree):1; full GMM(neighbor+degree):
  23; tied GMM:1. No selected GMM likelihood-decrease flags.
- PG: all720 converge;17 selected runs stop at tiny numerical plateaus, with
  no substantial numerical-decrease status. No empty hard clusters.
- Raw Bernoulli VEM:2 nonconverged,149 empty hard-cluster outputs,174 outputs
  with a hard cluster smaller than1%n. These are hard-label diagnostics, not
  proof that fitted soft mixture weights are zero.
- Spectral Bernoulli-shaped surrogate:8 nonconverged,79 empty hard-cluster
  outputs,98 small-cluster outputs,99 decoder fallbacks.
- Total decoder fallbacks107 across all8640 method/job combinations. Successful
  numerical completion is not evidence of a statistically safe inverse.

Interpretation constraints
---------------------------
The Gaussian and Poisson-Gaussian objectives are working/composite likelihoods,
not the original graph likelihood. The signed low-rank Bernoulli-shaped arm is
explicitly a surrogate. Only raw Bernoulli VEM reads original edges. No method
uses truth for restarting or fitting. Intervals are descriptive paired t
intervals with no multiplicity correction. Fixed n and a finite d grid do not
verify asymptotic error exponents; only d4,d6 are numerically below log1800.
Convergence diagnostics refer to selected restarts; complete trajectories of
all unsuccessful restarts are not saved by the runner.
